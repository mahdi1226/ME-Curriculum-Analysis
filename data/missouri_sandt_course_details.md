## Undergraduate Core Courses

### MECH ENG 1720 - Introduction to Engineering Design
- **Credits:** 2 lec + 1 lab
- **Prerequisites:** None
- **Description:** Systematic approach to design fundamentals including problem clarification, concept generation, concept selection, prototyping methods, engineering ethics, and communication techniques with CAD tools.

### MECH ENG 1761 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design
- **Credits:** 1 lab
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 1720; MATH 1211 or MATH 1214
- **Description:** Parametric sketching, solid modeling, assemblies, mass properties, engineering drawings, and file exchange principles.

### MECH ENG 2360 - Dynamics
- **Credits:** 3 lec
- **Prerequisites:** C or better in CIV ENG 2200 and MATH 2222
- **Description:** Comprehensive modeling using mechanics principles, covering particle kinematics, rigid body motion, and energy/momentum methods. Co-listed with Aero Eng 2360.

### MECH ENG 2519 - Thermodynamics
- **Credits:** 3 lec
- **Prerequisites:** C or better in MATH 1214 (or 1211), MATH 1215, MATH 2222, PHYSICS 1135 or 1111
- **Description:** Energy transformations and fundamental thermodynamic laws underlying engineering applications.

### MECH ENG 2653 - Introduction to Manufacturing Processes
- **Credits:** 2 lec + 1 lab
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 1720; C or better in PHYSICS 1135 or 1111
- **Co-requisites:** MECH ENG 1761 (preceding or concurrent)
- **Description:** Welding, joining, casting, forming, powder metallurgy and material removal with emphasis on capabilities and limitations.

### MECH ENG 2761 - Introduction to Mechanical Design
- **Credits:** 1 lec + 1 lab
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 1720, 1761, 2653; C or better in MATH 1214 (or 1211), PHYSICS 1135 or 1111
- **Co-requisites:** CIV ENG 2200 (preceding or concurrent)
- **Description:** Systematic design process emphasizing creativity, visualization, quality, failure analysis, and concurrent engineering through group projects.

### MECH ENG 3131 - Thermofluid Mechanics I
- **Credits:** 3 lec
- **Prerequisites:** C or better in MECH ENG 2519
- **Description:** Flow principles in ducts and nozzles, dimensional analysis, external and compressible flows, with applications to design.

### MECH ENG 3313 - Machine Dynamics
- **Credits:** 3 lec
- **Prerequisites:** C or better in Programming Elective, MECH ENG 2360, MATH 1214/1211, 1215, 2222, PHYSICS 1135
- **Description:** Vector-based motion analysis for machine elements including linkages, cams, and gears with dynamic force and balancing applications.

### MECH ENG 3411 - Modeling and Analysis of Dynamic Systems
- **Credits:** 3 lec
- **Prerequisites:** C or better in Programming Elective, MECH ENG 2360, MATH 1214/1211, 1215, 2222, 3304, PHYSICS 1135, 2135
- **Description:** Linear system modeling for hydraulic, pneumatic, and electromechanical systems using matrix formulations, Laplace transforms, and time domain response methods.

### MECH ENG 3521 - Applied Thermodynamics
- **Credits:** 3 lec
- **Prerequisites:** C or better in MECH ENG 2519
- **Description:** Advanced thermodynamic study: power and refrigeration cycles, gas mixtures, psychrometrics, behavior of real gases and combustion.

### MECH ENG 3525 - Heat Transfer
- **Credits:** 3 lec
- **Prerequisites:** C or better in Programming Elective, MATH 3304, MECH ENG 2519
- **Description:** Fundamentals of radiation, conduction, and convection heat transmission with engineering problem applications.

### MECH ENG 3708 - Machine Design I
- **Credits:** 3 lec
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 2653; MET ENG 2110 or AERO ENG 3877; C or better in CIV ENG 2210
- **Co-requisites:** MECH ENG 2761 (preceded or accompanied)
- **Description:** Machine element analysis covering shafts, springs, screws, belts, bearings, and gears with fatigue treatment and finite element introduction.

### MECH ENG 4479 - Automatic Control of Dynamic Systems
- **Credits:** 3 lec
- **Prerequisites:** C or better in MECH ENG 3411
- **Description:** Classical control methods including root locus, Bode plots, and Nyquist diagrams applied to mechanical system design.

### MECH ENG 4480 - Control System Laboratory
- **Credits:** 1 lab
- **Co-requisites:** MECH ENG 4479 (preceding or concurrent)
- **Description:** Experimental data acquisition and system control with emphasis on computer-based measurement and control.

### MECH ENG 4761 - Engineering Design (Capstone)
- **Credits:** 1 lec + 2 lab
- **Co-requisites:** MECH ENG 3708, 3525, 3131, 4479 (all concurrent)
- **Description:** Real-life design projects emphasizing problem definition, conceptualization, modeling, approximation techniques and optimization with teamwork.

### MECH ENG 4840 - Mechanical Instrumentation
- **Credits:** 2 lab
- **Prerequisites:** C or better in MATH 3304, MECH ENG 2519, PHYSICS 2135 or 2111
- **Description:** Theory and application of instrumentation for measuring strain, pressure, force, temperature, motion, flow, and sound.

### MECH ENG 4842 - Mechanical Engineering Systems
- **Credits:** 2 lab
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 4840, 3521, 3131, 3525, 3313
- **Description:** Laboratory evaluating complete mechanical systems with analysis of mechanical and thermodynamic performance.

## Popular 5000-Level Technical Electives

### MECH ENG 5131 - Intermediate Thermofluid Mechanics
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 3131 or AERO ENG 3131
- **Description:** Navier-Stokes equations, boundary layer theory, micro-fluidics, numerical methods.

### MECH ENG 5212 - Introduction to Finite Element Analysis
- **Prerequisites:** MATH 3304; senior or graduate standing
- **Description:** Variational formulation and FEA modeling for mechanics, fluids, and heat transfer.

### MECH ENG 5307 - Vibrations I
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 3411 and 3313, or AERO ENG 3613 and MATH 3304
- **Description:** Single and multidegree freedom systems, natural frequencies, resonance, continuous systems.

### MECH ENG 5449 - Robotic Manipulators and Mechanisms
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 3313; Programming Elective
- **Description:** Industrial robotics covering kinematics, dynamics, control, and machine vision.

### MECH ENG 5478 - Mechatronics
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 4479 or equivalent
- **Description:** Integration of mechanical, electrical, computer, and control systems.

### MECH ENG 5525 - Intermediate Heat Transfer
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 3525
- **Description:** Analytical conduction, radiation theory, and convective heat transfer.

### MECH ENG 5533 - Internal Combustion Engines
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 3521
- **Description:** Spark and compression ignition engines.

### MECH ENG 5709 - Machine Design II
- **Prerequisites:** MECH ENG 3708
- **Description:** Advanced machine elements: bearings, gears, stress analysis.

### MECH ENG 5830 - Applied Computational Methods
- **Prerequisites:** Programming Elective, MATH 3304
- **Description:** Computational methods for fluids, structures, thermodynamics, and controls.
