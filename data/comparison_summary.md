## Key Observations for UMSL Program Design

### ABET Requirements (all three meet these):
1. **Math & Basic Science:** Min 32 credit hours (Calc I-III, DiffEq, Physics I-II, Chemistry)
2. **Engineering Topics:** Min 48 credit hours including design
3. **General Education:** Breadth component
4. **Capstone Design:** Integrating experience
5. **Lab Experience:** Hands-on throughout curriculum

### Common Core Across All Three (Must-Have for UMSL):
- Calculus I, II, III + Differential Equations
- Physics I, II (calculus-based)
- Chemistry (1 semester minimum)
- Statics → Dynamics → Mechanics of Solids
- Thermodynamics (at least 1 course, ideally 2)
- Fluid Mechanics
- Heat Transfer
- Materials Science
- Machine Design
- Dynamic Systems / Controls
- Electrical Circuits
- Programming/Computing
- Capstone Design (2-semester preferred for ABET)
- Engineering Economics or Management

### Differentiators to Consider for UMSL:
1. **Missouri S&T strength:** Deep prerequisite enforcement (C or better), strong lab sequence, emphasis areas
2. **SIUE strength:** Clean 4-year path, balanced credit loads, ethics course explicit
3. **SLU strength:** Early design exposure (Year 1), mechatronics integration, modern topics (PLC/robotics)

### Recommended UMSL Approach:
- Follow SIUE's clean credit distribution (14-18 per semester)
- Adopt Missouri S&T's prerequisite rigor (C or better in gateway courses)
- Include SLU's early design/hands-on philosophy
- 2-semester capstone (stronger for ABET)
- Include explicit ethics course
- Include FE exam requirement
- Target 128 credit hours total
