# PART 1: ABET COMPLIANCE IMPROVEMENTS

## A. Critical Gaps (Must Fix for ABET)

### 1. Kinematics/Dynamics of Mechanisms — MISSING
**ABET ME Program Criteria** requires coverage of both thermal AND mechanical systems.
Mechanisms/kinematics is a core mechanical systems topic.

| School | Course | Credits |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Missouri S&T | MECH ENG 3313 Machine Dynamics | 3 |
| SIUE | ME 350 Dynamics of Mechanisms | 3 |
| SLU | (integrated into dynamics/design) | - |
| **UMSL** | **NONE** | **0** |

**Recommendation:** Add **MENG 33XX Kinematics and Dynamics of Mechanisms** (3 credits)
- Content: Linkage analysis, cam design, gear trains, balancing, mechanism synthesis
- Prerequisites: ENGR 2320 (Dynamics), MATH 2020
- Place in: Year 3 Fall (alongside or replacing a fixed advanced course)
- This is arguably the single biggest ABET gap

### 2. Vibrations — MISSING
Part of ABET "mechanical systems" breadth requirement.

| School | Course | Credits |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Missouri S&T | MECH ENG 5307 Vibrations I (elective) | 3 |
| SIUE | (covered within ME 356 Dynamic Systems) | - |
| SLU | MENG 3110 Linear Vibrations | 3 |
| **UMSL** | **NONE** | **0** |

**Recommendation Options:**
- **Option A (Minimal):** Add vibrations module (2-3 weeks) into MENG 3350 System Dynamics and Control — rename to "System Dynamics, Vibrations, and Control"
- **Option B (Better):** Add **MENG 33XX Mechanical Vibrations** (3 credits) as a required or elective course
- Option A is faster to implement; Option B is stronger for ABET

### 3. Limited Technical Elective Flexibility
Current UMSL program has 4 fixed upper-division ME courses (4440, 4450, 4460, 4490) with NO student choice. ABET evaluators look for depth AND breadth — fixed courses suggest limited depth options.

| School | Elective Structure |
|--------|-------------------|
| Missouri S&T | 9 hrs technical electives (6 ME + 3 breadth) with 20+ options |
| SIUE | 9 hrs ME electives + 3 hrs engineering elective from menu |
| SLU | 12 hrs technical electives (4 courses) from menu |
| **UMSL** | **0 hrs electives — all fixed** |

**Recommendation:** Convert 2-3 of the fixed courses (4440, 4450, 4460, 4490) into an elective menu:
- Keep 1-2 as required (e.g., keep MENG 4490 Advanced Manufacturing as required)
- Create 6-9 credits of "ME Technical Elective" slots
- Offer 4440, 4450, 4460 + new courses (Mechanisms, Vibrations, FEA, Mechatronics) as options
- This also allows faculty to develop specialty courses over time

## B. Important Improvements (Strengthen ABET Case)

### 3.5 Catalog vs. 4-Year Plan Inconsistency (EENG 2310)
**Problem:** EENG 2310 Circuit Analysis I catalog entry states prerequisites are:
- MATH 1900 (Calc II)
- PHYSICS 2111/2111L (Physics I)
- **Concurrent enrollment in MATH 2020** (Differential Equations)

However, the 4-year plan places:
- EENG 2310 in Y2 Fall
- MATH 2020 in Y2 Spring (one semester LATER)

A student following the 4-year plan literally cannot satisfy the concurrent MATH 2020 requirement. ABET evaluators check exactly this kind of catalog-vs-sequence consistency.

**Options to resolve:**
- Move MATH 2020 to Y2 Fall (alongside MATH 2000), or
- Move EENG 2310 to Y2 Spring, or
- Update the EENG 2310 catalog entry to remove the concurrent requirement

**Recommendation:** Move MATH 2020 to Y2 Fall. This also better supports MENG 3350 and MENG 3370 prerequisites in Y3 Fall.

### 4. Capstone Design Credits
UMSL capstone is 2+2=4 credits. Peers:
- S&T: 3 credits (2-semester)
- SIUE: 3+3=6 credits
- SLU: 3 credits (1-semester)

**Recommendation:** Consider increasing to 3+3=6 credits or at minimum 2+3=5.
The capstone must demonstrate integration of multiple ME areas with realistic constraints.
More credits = more time for meaningful design work = stronger ABET evidence.

### 5. Prerequisite Enforcement (Grade Requirements)
Missouri S&T requires **C or better** in 14 gateway courses. UMSL's bulletin does not mention specific grade requirements for progression.

**Recommendation:** Establish explicit C or better requirements in:
- MATH 1800, 1900, 2000, 2020 (Calc sequence + DiffEq)
- PHYSICS 2111, 2112
- ENGR 2310, 2320, 2330, 2332 (Statics, Dynamics, Thermo, Mechanics of Materials)
- EENG 2310 (Circuits)
- CMP SCI 1250 (Computing)

This demonstrates program rigor to ABET evaluators.

### 6. Numerical Methods / Computational Tools
ABET Criterion 5 requires students use "modern engineering tools." A dedicated numerical methods course strengthens this.

| School | Course |
|--------|--------|
| SIUE | ME 354 Numerical Simulation (1 cr) |
| Missouri S&T | MECH ENG 5830 Applied Computational Methods (elective) |
| **UMSL** | CMP SCI 1250 (general intro) + MENG 4450 CFD (application) |

**Recommendation:** Either:
- Add a 1-credit **MENG 2XXX Computational Methods for Engineers** (MATLAB/Python for ME applications)
- OR integrate MATLAB/Python modules into MENG 3350 and MENG 3370

### 7. ENGR 3300 Prerequisite Error
The bulletin lists EENG 2310 (Circuit Analysis) as prerequisite for ENGR 3300 (Applied Thermodynamics). This is almost certainly wrong — should be ENGR 2330 (Intro to Thermodynamics).

**Recommendation:** Fix in next catalog revision. ABET evaluators will catch this.

## C. Assessment Infrastructure (ABET Criterion 4)

### 8. Student Outcome Assessment Plan
For initial ABET accreditation, UMSL needs a documented continuous improvement process:

**Required elements:**
- Course-level assessment rubrics mapping to SOs 1-7
- Direct measures: FE exam pass rates, capstone design evaluation rubrics, exam embedded questions
- Indirect measures: exit surveys, employer surveys, alumni surveys (3-5 year post-graduation)
- Annual assessment cycle with documented "closing the loop" actions
- Advisory board (industry members) reviewing PEOs periodically

**Recommendation:** Start collecting assessment data NOW even before ABET visit. You need at least 2 years of data showing continuous improvement.

# PART 2: AI-ERA CURRICULUM REVISIONS

## The Landscape

ABET's official AI policy (2025) is deliberately **non-prescriptive**: they will not dictate how programs use AI, but they note that accreditation commissions "may add curricular topics related to AI" on a discipline-by-discipline basis. The EAC has proposed Criterion 5 curriculum changes for 2026-2027 — likely to address modern computational tools including AI.

This means UMSL has a **first-mover advantage**: as a brand-new program, it can integrate AI thoughtfully before ABET mandates it, positioning itself as a leader rather than a follower.

## Courses That Need AI-Era Revision

### 1. PHIL 2259 — Engineering Ethics (3 credits)
**Current state:** Traditional engineering ethics (safety, codes, whistleblowing, professional responsibility)

**What needs to change:**
- **AI decision-making ethics:** When an AI-designed component fails, who is liable — the engineer who approved it, the company that trained the model, or the software vendor?
- **Algorithmic bias in engineering:** AI tools trained on historical data can perpetuate design biases (e.g., crash test dummies modeled on male bodies)
- **Responsible AI use in practice:** When is it appropriate to use AI-generated designs? What verification is required?
- **Autonomous systems ethics:** Self-driving vehicles, autonomous manufacturing, drone operations
- **Data privacy in IoT/smart systems:** Mechanical systems increasingly collect data — ethical obligations
- **Environmental justice of AI:** Energy cost of training models, sustainability implications
- **IP and attribution:** Using AI-generated code/designs — copyright and professional standards

**Recommendation:** Rename to **"Engineering Ethics in the AI Era"** or keep name but add 3-4 weeks of AI-specific content. Should include case studies of AI failures in engineering contexts.

### 2. CMP SCI 1250 — Introduction to Computing (3 credits)
**Current state:** General intro to computing/programming

**What needs to change:**
- **Python-first approach** (industry standard for ME + AI/ML)
- **Data science fundamentals:** Data cleaning, visualization, basic statistics with code
- **Introduction to ML concepts:** What is supervised/unsupervised learning, when to use it
- **AI tools for engineers:** Using LLMs for code generation, GitHub Copilot, prompt engineering for engineering tasks
- **Computational thinking for ME:** Applying programming to solve ME problems (stress analysis scripts, thermal simulation, data from sensors)

**Recommendation:** Revise to **"Computing and Data Science for Engineers"** with Python, pandas, basic ML concepts. Keep it practical — students should leave able to write a script that analyzes experimental data and fits a model.

### 3. MENG 3350 — System Dynamics and Control (3+1 credits)
**What needs to change:**
- **AI-based control:** Introduction to ML-based controllers, reinforcement learning concepts for control
- **Digital twins:** Modeling physical systems with computational models that update in real-time
- **Data-driven modeling:** System identification from sensor data using ML rather than first-principles only
- **Simulation tools:** MATLAB/Simulink + Python control libraries

### 4. MENG 3360 — Machine Design and Manufacturing (3 credits)
**What needs to change:**
- **Generative/topology-optimized design:** AI-driven design optimization tools (e.g., Autodesk Generative Design, ANSYS topology optimization)
- **AI-assisted CAD:** Using AI tools within SolidWorks, Fusion 360 for design suggestions
- **Design for additive manufacturing:** AI-optimized lattice structures, support generation
- **AI in quality control:** Machine vision for inspection, statistical process control with ML

### 5. MENG 4490 — Advanced Manufacturing (3 credits)
**What needs to change:**
- **Smart manufacturing / Industry 4.0:** IoT sensors, digital twins, predictive maintenance using ML
- **AI-driven process optimization:** Using ML to optimize CNC parameters, 3D printing settings
- **Robotics and automation with AI:** Collaborative robots (cobots), computer vision for inspection
- **Supply chain AI:** Demand forecasting, just-in-time with ML

### 6. MENG 4450 — Computational Fluid Dynamics (3 credits)
**What needs to change:**
- **Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs):** ML models that incorporate governing equations
- **ML surrogate models:** Training neural networks to replace expensive CFD simulations
- **Data-driven turbulence modeling:** Modern approaches using ML to improve RANS/LES models
- **Cloud computing for CFD:** Running simulations on AWS/Azure/HPC clusters

### 7. ENGR 1414 — Elementary Engineering Design (2 credits)
**What needs to change:**
- **AI literacy for engineers:** What AI can and cannot do, when to trust AI outputs
- **Prompt engineering for design:** Using LLMs to brainstorm, generate specifications, research prior art
- **Critical evaluation of AI outputs:** Engineers must verify AI-generated solutions

### 8. MENG 4980/4990 — Senior Design Capstone (2+2 credits)
**What needs to change:**
- **AI tool policy:** Clear guidelines on acceptable AI use in capstone (must be documented, verified)
- **AI as design tool:** Students should be expected to evaluate AI tools as part of their design process
- **Ethical review:** Capstone should include an ethical analysis section covering AI implications of the design

## New Courses to Consider

### A. MENG 4XXX — AI and Machine Learning for Mechanical Engineers (3 credits, elective)
**Content:**
- Supervised/unsupervised learning with engineering datasets
- Neural networks for regression and classification of ME problems
- Physics-informed machine learning
- Computer vision for inspection and quality control
- Predictive maintenance and condition monitoring
- Reinforcement learning for control and optimization
- Hands-on projects: predict material properties from composition, optimize thermal systems, classify manufacturing defects

**Prerequisites:** CMP SCI 1250, MATH 1320 (Stats), Junior standing
**Justification:** Missouri S&T already has MECH ENG 5479 (ML for Manufacturing Automation). This would be UMSL's equivalent, broader in scope.

### B. MENG 3XXX — Mechatronics and Smart Systems (3 credits, elective)
**Content:**
- Sensors, actuators, microcontrollers (Arduino/Raspberry Pi)
- IoT for mechanical systems
- Basic computer vision
- Embedded AI (TinyML, edge computing)
- Integration of mechanical, electrical, and software systems

**Prerequisites:** EENG 2310, ENGR 2320
**Justification:** SLU has MENG 2400 (Mechatronics). S&T has MECH ENG 5478 (Mechatronics). UMSL has nothing in this space. This is increasingly essential for modern ME practice.
