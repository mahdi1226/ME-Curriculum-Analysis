## Emphasis Areas (Optional)
1. **Energy Conversion** - 4 approved courses
2. **Manufacturing Processes** - MECH ENG 3653 + 3 electives
3. **Mechanical Design & Analysis** - 1 design + 1 analysis + 2 additional
4. **Systems Integration** - Missouri State co-op program

## Key Notes
- FE Exam required before graduation (passing not required)
- Must earn C or better in: CHEM 1305, MATH 1214/1211, 1215, 2222, 3304, PHYSICS 1135, 2135, CIV ENG 2200, 2210, MECH ENG 2519, 2360, 3411, MET ENG 2110, Programming Elective
- 2.0 GPA required overall and in ME courses
