## Degree Requirements
- Cumulative GPA of 2.0
- Term GPA above 1.0
- 2.0 GPA in math/science courses
- 2.0 GPA in engineering courses
- 2.0 GPA in ME courses numbered 300+
- No more than 2 failures/incompletes/withdrawals in any single required course

## Required Course Categories
- **Physical Science Foundation:** CHEM 131/135, MATH 152/250/305, PHYS 141/151L/142/152L
- **Engineering Foundation:** CE 204, CE 240, CE 242, IE 106, IE 345, ECE 210, CS 145
- **ME Core:** ME 262, 310, 312, 315, 350, 354, 356, 356L, 370, 380, 380L, 410, 410L, 482, 484
- **ME Electives:** 9 credit hours
- **Engineering Elective:** 3 credit hours
