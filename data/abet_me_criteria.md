# ABET EAC Criteria for Mechanical Engineering Programs (2025-2026)
## Source: abet.org

## 8 General Criteria

### Criterion 1 - Students
- Evaluate student performance, monitor progress toward outcomes
- Provide academic advising
- Enforce policies for admissions, transfer credits, graduation

### Criterion 2 - Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
- Published objectives aligned with institutional mission
- Systematic periodic review processes
- PEOs describe expected accomplishments 3-5 years after graduation

### Criterion 3 - Student Outcomes (SOs)
Programs must demonstrate 7 student outcomes at graduation:

| SO | Description | Key Courses (UMSL mapping) |
|----|-------------|---------------------------|
| 1 | Identify, formulate, and solve complex engineering problems using engineering, science, and math | Statics, Dynamics, Thermo, Fluids, Heat Transfer, Controls |
| 2 | Apply engineering design to produce solutions considering public health, safety, welfare, global, cultural, social, environmental, economic factors | Capstone (MENG 4980/4990), Machine Design (MENG 3360) |
| 3 | Communicate effectively with a range of audiences | Technical Writing (ENGL 3130), Capstone presentations |
| 4 | Recognize ethical and professional responsibilities, make informed judgments considering impact | Engineering Ethics (PHIL 2259) |
| 5 | Function effectively on a team providing leadership and collaborative environment | Capstone, Lab courses, ENGR 1414 |
| 6 | Develop and conduct appropriate experimentation, analyze and interpret data, use engineering judgment | Labs (3370L, 3380L, 3350L, 3330), MENG 4460 |
| 7 | Acquire and apply new knowledge using appropriate learning strategies | Self-directed learning in electives, senior design |

### Criterion 4 - Continuous Improvement
- Regularly assess SO attainment using documented processes
- Use results to drive program improvements
- Requires assessment tools: exams, projects, surveys, FE exam pass rates

### Criterion 5 - Curriculum
**Minimum Requirements:**
- **30 semester credit hours** of college-level mathematics and basic sciences (with experimental experience)
- **45 semester credit hours** of engineering topics (engineering sciences + engineering design)
- Broad education (general education) component
- Culminating **major design experience** (capstone) incorporating:
  - Engineering standards and realistic constraints
  - Multiple design constraints (economic, environmental, social, political, ethical, health/safety, manufacturability, sustainability)
  - Knowledge from prior coursework

### Criterion 6 - Faculty
- Sufficient faculty covering all curricular areas
- Faculty must have appropriate qualifications
- Student-faculty interaction, advising, professional development
- PE licensure valued

### Criterion 7 - Facilities
- Adequate classrooms, labs, equipment, computing
- Maintained and upgraded systematically

### Criterion 8 - Institutional Support
- Sufficient resources for quality
- Attract qualified faculty
- Maintain infrastructure
- Respectful environment

## Program Criteria: Mechanical and Similarly Named Engineering Programs

**Lead Society:** American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME)

### Required Curriculum Content:
1. **Foundational Topics:** Principles of engineering, basic science, and mathematics including **multivariate calculus** and **differential equations**
2. **Applied Learning:** Applications to **modeling, analysis, design, and realization** of physical systems, components, or processes
3. **Breadth Requirement:** Must cover BOTH **thermal systems** AND **mechanical systems**
4. **Depth Requirement:** In-depth coverage of EITHER thermal OR mechanical systems (or both)

### What "Thermal Systems" Covers:
- Thermodynamics
- Fluid mechanics / fluid dynamics
- Heat transfer
- Energy systems
- HVAC/Refrigeration (optional depth)

### What "Mechanical Systems" Covers:
- Statics and dynamics
- Mechanics of materials / stress analysis
- Machine design
- Kinematics and dynamics of mechanisms
- Vibrations
- Controls / dynamic systems
- Manufacturing processes

### Faculty Requirements:
- Faculty for upper-level ME courses must maintain current knowledge in specialty areas

## ABET GAP ANALYSIS: UMSL vs. Criteria

### GAPS (Potential Issues for Accreditation):

1. **Kinematics/Dynamics of Mechanisms** - MISSING
   - ABET ME criteria require coverage of mechanical systems including mechanisms
   - S&T has MECH ENG 3313 (Machine Dynamics)
   - SIUE has ME 350 (Dynamics of Mechanisms)
   - SLU integrates into other courses
   - **UMSL has no equivalent** - this is a significant gap

2. **Vibrations** - MISSING
   - Part of "mechanical systems" breadth requirement
   - S&T offers as 5000-level elective (5307)
   - SLU has MENG 3110 (Linear Vibrations)
   - SIUE covers through dynamic systems modeling
   - **UMSL has no equivalent** - may need to add or integrate into MENG 3350

3. **Limited Technical Elective Flexibility**
   - Only 4 fixed upper-division ME courses (4440, 4450, 4460, 4490)
   - No elective choice for students
   - Peers offer 3-4 elective slots with course menus
   - **Concern:** ABET evaluators may note lack of depth options

4. **Second Thermodynamics Course**
   - ENGR 3300 (Applied Thermo) helps, but listed under EENG 2310 prerequisite (possibly an error)
   - S&T and SIUE both have clear Thermo I → Thermo II sequences
   - **Minor concern** - currently adequate but prerequisite chain unclear

### STRENGTHS (Exceeds Minimum):
1. Strong lab integration (5 lab courses)
2. 2-semester capstone design
3. FE Exam review course
4. Dedicated ethics course
5. Modern topics (CFD, Composites, Renewable Energy, Adv. Manufacturing)
6. 35 hrs math/science (5 above minimum)
7. 65 hrs engineering topics (20 above minimum)

### RECOMMENDATIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT:
1. **Add Mechanisms course** or integrate kinematics/dynamics of mechanisms into existing course
2. **Add Vibrations** content - could be a module in MENG 3350 or a new course
3. **Add 2-3 technical elective slots** replacing fixed courses (e.g., make 4440/4450 elective choices)
4. **Clarify ENGR 3300 prerequisite** (should be ENGR 2330, not EENG 2310)
5. Consider adding a **numerical methods** course (SIUE has ME 354)
