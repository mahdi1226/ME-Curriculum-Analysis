## University Undergraduate Core: 32-35 credits
SLU's Ignatian Core includes:
- CORE 1500: Theological Foundations
- CORE 1600: Philosophical Foundations
- CORE 1700: Historical Knowledge
- CORE 1200: Literary Analysis
- CORE 1900: Social & Behavioral Sciences
- CORE 3400: Ethics and Social Analysis
- CORE 3600: Faith & Reason
- Equity/Global requirements

## Degree Requirements
- Minimum 2.00 cumulative GPA
- MATH 1510 and 1520 require C- or better
- ABET accredited

## Key Distinguishing Features
- Early emphasis on design (MENG 1000 Design Thinking in Year 1)
- Mechatronics integration (MENG 2400 in Year 2)
- PLC & Robotics course (MENG 4450)
- Strong liberal arts core (Jesuit tradition - 32-35 credits)
- Engineering Experimentation early (Year 2)
- Single capstone course (MENG 4024) rather than two-semester sequence
