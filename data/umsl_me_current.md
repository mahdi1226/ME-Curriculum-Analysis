## KEY OBSERVATIONS

### Strengths of Current UMSL Program:
1. Clean 125-credit total (lower than competitors)
2. Integrated lab experiences (3370L, 3380L, 3350L)
3. Modern topics: Composites (4440), CFD (4450), Renewable Energy (4460), Advanced Mfg (4490)
4. 2-semester capstone
5. FE Exam review course
6. Engineering Ethics as dedicated course
7. Research/Entrepreneurship track available

### Potential Gaps vs. Competitors:
1. **No dedicated Mechanisms/Machine Dynamics course** (S&T has 3313, SIUE has ME 350)
2. **No vibrations course** (S&T has 5307 elective, SLU has MENG 3110)
3. **Only 1 Thermo course** + Applied Thermo (S&T and SIUE have 2 distinct thermo courses)
4. **No explicit numerical methods/simulation** course (SIUE has ME 354)
5. **Fewer technical elective choices** (only 4440, 4450, 4460, 4490 as ME-specific upper division)
6. **No mechatronics** (SLU has MENG 2400)
7. **Math credits heavier** (5+5+5+3 = 18 for calc sequence vs 4+4+4+3 = 15 at others)
8. **125 total vs 128** at peers - has room for 3 more credits

### ABET Compliance Notes:
- Program is NEW (2025) - likely pursuing initial ABET accreditation
- Cannot receive ABET until first class graduates and a self-study is completed
- Typical timeline: ~2-3 years after first graduates
- Curriculum appears to meet ABET student outcomes but needs formal mapping
- Missing "Mechanical and Similarly Named" program criteria items:
  - Kinematics/dynamics of mechanisms
  - Vibrations
