Coverage Summary

All 7 Student Outcomes are addressed by multiple courses. SO 1 (problem solving) has the strongest coverage at 17 courses. SO 3 (communication), SO 4 (ethics), and SO 5 (teamwork) have the minimum coverage at 4 courses each — these could be strengthened by adding communication/teamwork components to more lab courses.
