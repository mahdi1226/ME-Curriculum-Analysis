ABET Gap Resolution

The current UMSL ME program has three critical ABET gaps and one recommended policy addition. Below is the before/after status for each.

Gap 1: Kinematics/Dynamics of Mechanisms

BEFORE No course covering linkage analysis, cam design, gear trains, or mechanism synthesis. S&T and SIUE both have dedicated courses.

AFTER FIXED - Add MENG 33XX Kinematics & Dynamics of Mechanisms (3 cr) in Year 3 Fall. Covers linkage analysis, cam design, gear trains, balancing, and mechanism synthesis.

Gap 2: Vibrations

BEFORE No vibrations coverage anywhere in the curriculum. ABET ME program criteria requires mechanical systems breadth including vibrations.

AFTER FIXED - Expand MENG 3350 to include vibrations content (2-3 weeks). Rename to "System Dynamics, Vibrations & Control." Follows SIUE model of integrating vibrations into the dynamics/control course.

Gap 3: No Technical Electives

BEFORE All four upper-division ME courses (4440, 4450, 4460, 4490) are fixed and required. Zero elective choice. Peers offer 6-12 credits of electives from menus of 10-20+ courses.

AFTER FIXED - Convert MENG 4440 and MENG 4460 to elective options. Create 6-credit ME Elective menu with 5 choices (expanding over time). Students select 2 courses from the menu.

Gap 4: Prerequisite Grade Requirements

BEFORE No explicit grade requirements for course progression. S&T requires C or better in 14 gateway courses.

RECOMMENDED Add C-or-better policy for 12 gateway courses: MATH 1800/1900/2000/2020, PHYS 2111/2112, ENGR 2310/2320/2330/2332, EENG 2310, CMP SCI 1250. Demonstrates program rigor to ABET evaluators.

Load improvement: The current plan has an 18-credit overload in Year 3 Spring. The proposed revision reduces this to 16 credits by moving Technical Writing (ENGL 3130) to Year 3 Spring from Year 3 Fall (which absorbs the new Mechanisms course), and redistributing MENG 3390 and one Gen Ed to Year 4. Implementation Timeline

A phased rollout minimizes disruption to currently enrolled students while progressively building toward full ABET compliance before the first graduating class.

Phase 1 - Fall 2026
Content Updates (Low Effort)
  • Update PHIL 2259 with AI ethics modules (3-4 weeks of new content)
  • Update CMP SCI 1250 to Python-first with data science fundamentals
  • Update ENGR 1414 with AI literacy module
  • Establish C-or-better prerequisite policy for gateway courses
Phase 2 - Fall 2027
New Course + Curriculum Restructure (Critical ABET Fixes)
  • Launch MENG 33XX Kinematics & Dynamics of Mechanisms (3 cr, new course)
  • Expand MENG 3350 with vibrations content; rename to "System Dynamics, Vibrations & Control"
  • Implement revised semester sequencing (move Tech Writing, redistribute loads)
  • Update MENG 3360 with generative design content
Phase 3 - Fall 2028
Elective Menu Launch
  • Convert MENG 4440 and MENG 4460 from required to elective menu options
  • Expand MENG 4460 from 1 cr lab to 3 cr course (Renewable Energy Systems + Lab)
  • Launch first cohort of ME Elective choices
  • Update MENG 4490 with Industry 4.0 content
  • Update MENG 4450 with PINNs and ML surrogate models
Phase 4 - Fall 2029
Expansion + ABET Preparation
  • Add MENG 4XXX AI/ML for Mechanical Engineers elective (3 cr)
  • Add MENG 3XXX Mechatronics & Smart Systems elective (3 cr)
  • First graduating class completes the program
  • Begin ABET self-study preparation with 2+ years of assessment data
  • Compile continuous improvement evidence for ABET evaluation visit
Timeline alignment: The first UMSL ME graduating class is expected around 2029. ABET requires at least one graduating class before a program can apply for initial accreditation. This phased plan ensures all critical gaps are fixed well before the ABET self-study begins, giving the program 2+ years of assessment data to demonstrate continuous improvement.
